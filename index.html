Gym Membership System

This semester, I worked on a group project called Gym Membership and Class Booking System as part of my coursework.

Our goal was to develop a user-friendly web application that makes managing memberships and scheduling sessions at the gym easier.
Members of the gym can conveniently register, verify class schedules, schedule sessions ahead of time, and monitor their attendance using our solution.
To keep members informed about their bookings and membership status, the system also automatically sends notifications, which enhances user engagement and optimizes operations.

The platform gives gym administrators the administrative resources they need to effectively organize classes, keep track of attendance, and manage member profiles.
By adding PayPal, it also improves payment processing, making class payments and membership renewals easy.
We use AWS as our cloud platform to guarantee that the system can scale as needed, and we've integrated Twilio to manage real-time notifications for significant developments.

This project helped me dive deep into full-stack development.
I contributed to both the frontend (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) and backend (Node.js) development, as well as database management using PostgreSQL.
One of the most rewarding aspects was successfully integrating payment and notification systems, ensuring both security and a smooth user experience.

Through this project, I’ve not only improved my technical skills but also learned the importance of collaboration and communication in a team.
It’s been an excellent opportunity to see how a real-world application can come together from concept to deployment.
